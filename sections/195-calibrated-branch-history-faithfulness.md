\[
T:V\times V\longrightarrow V\times V.
\]

Supply two exact local laws

\[
T(x,x)=(x,x),\qquad T(T(x,y))=(y,x).
\]

The existing transparent swap-root reconstruction extracts additive endomorphisms `A` and `B` with

\[
T(x,y)=(Ax+By,\,Bx+Ay).
\]

It already derives additive inverses, a genuine half, antipodal cancellation, and phase structure. This algebraic reconstruction is prior work reused by the present cluster. [TransparentSwapRoot.lean](../Proofs/QMClosure/TransparentSwapRoot.lean)

The smaller response contract assigns a real number `W(s)` to each finite word `s` in `A` and `B`. Its supplied laws are

2. Equal word endomorphisms have equal weights.
3. One raw native column is normalized, `W(A)+W(B)=1`.

The response domain consists of serial branch histories. Response values on arbitrary additive operator combinations remain unspecified. The contract supplies the real codomain and one column normalization. Unit weight, branch positivity, and characteristic zero are conclusions of the operator result. ## Faithful generated operator arithmetic

The operator theorem derives

\[
W(\varepsilon)=1,\qquad W(A)=W(B)=\tfrac12,\qquad
W(s)=2^{-|s|}.
\]

Here `ε` is the empty serial word, whose action is the identity. Consequently, equal word actions have equal lengths. The derived half `H=AB` has distinguishable serial powers. These facts exclude finite characteristic in the actual endomorphism ring.

The local reconstruction also gives

\[
AB=BA,\qquad 2H=I,\qquad U=2A^2,\qquad U^2=-I,
\]

\[
A=H(I+U),\qquad B=H(I-U).
\]

The subring generated by `A` and `B` is commutative and has faithful Gaussian-dyadic normal forms

\[
H^k(mI+nU),\qquad k\in\mathbb N,\quad m,n\in\mathbb Z.
\]

Two presentations satisfy exactly

\[
H^k(mI+nU)=H^\ell(rI+sU)
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
2^\ell m=2^k r\ \text{and}\ 2^\ell n=2^k s.
\]

The canonical conjugation is an additive, multiplicative involution on this generated operator image. It fixes `H`, sends `U` to `U³`, and exchanges `A` and `B`. Reflection of a branch word preserves its derived weight. The surrounding endomorphism ring and general circuit composition retain their own potentially noncommutative structure.
