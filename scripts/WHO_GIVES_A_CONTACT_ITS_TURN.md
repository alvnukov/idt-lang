# Who gives a contact its turn?

The [half-filled quartet](WHEN_THIRDS_ARE_ACTUALLY_BUILT.md) gives an exact count transformation at one junction. Connecting two such junctions asks three different questions: who fires, what a shared place means, and what passes from one firing to the next.

**A turn can be passed without an external alternating list.** Take six bits (a,b,c,d,h,g) and let H act on (a,b,c,h) and G on (b,c,d,g). A legal firing complements two occupied and two empty places. Of the 64 states, eight support H,G,H; in these, each of the pairs (b,c), (a,h), (d,g) contains one mark. Give H permission when b=0 and G permission when b=1. The active device itself flips b, yielding two four-event cycles. No further changing bit is needed. But this supplies the oppositely interpreted grant, and H and G each see only one shared pair pattern at their own firing. This is a clock for a restricted closed system, not transmission of arbitrary input.

**Waiting does not repair a misordered input.** In the open version, H sends two outputs and an independent third wire supplies the next bit of G. If w is the omitted H output, s the independent bit, and h,g the two current mirror bits, the next row is admissible exactly when s−w=1−h−g. A pause does not change this condition. With freely chosen initial G phase, 1 of 6, 69 of 90, and 1,747 of 1,860 balanced input histories of length 2, 4, and 6 cannot pass in arrival order. Connecting s=w and choosing opposite phases does pass every legal H input, but sends back a fixed permutation of its bits. [Exhaustive grant and queue check](probe_raw_contact_grant_fifo.py).

**The meaning of a shared bit affects the next observable.** On a triangle with two edges per side, prepare all three four-place contacts ready. When both ends read the same occupation, all eight such preparations leave all three ready after any one firing. When endpoint readings are opposite, two of ten ready preparations leave only the just-fired contact ready; the others behave like the first model. On a simple five-vertex graph even the common-bit model admits 120 of 252 half-filled states where different first choices give different numbers of next offers. Each individual firing can be undone: an order-sensitive observation is not a failure of branches to rejoin. A simultaneous write and a sequence of two flips are different operations. [Edge-frame and order checks](probe_contact_edge_frames.py).

**Do outside marks choose a pairing?** Give each port the count of occupied *other* edges at its neighboring vertex. Rule T exchanges the bits of every pair of ports with the same count when its class has exactly two members. Rule F also exchanges the two singleton-class ports if there are exactly two. Both require the center to be half filled. Only equality of the counts matters, not the names of the ports or an ordering of their scores. Each rule is locally reversible, and in all 4,096 local controls they offer precisely the same nontrivial events. This is an additional neighborhood-reading rule; which offered event is taken remains unspecified.

On the five-vertex complete graph at half filling their outputs agree. On the six-vertex octahedron they offer the same moves at a given input but can produce different histories: T changes the parity of two triangular circuits in 384 of 864 directed events; F never does. For F a nontrivial event flips the full incident star, which meets any simple circuit in zero or two edges. One two-event T history replaces one odd triangle by another while preserving three others, via odd-triangle counts 4→2→4. The positions refer to the *same persistent graph*: its endpoints considered separately are isomorphic. Conversely none of the 168 two-odd starts reaches a different two-odd pattern under T on this graph. The two-event example was found in the census, not predicted before it. An odd circuit is a chosen observable, not an identified particle. [Neighbor rules and exact reachability](probe_neighbor_twins_flux.py).

These are finite computations on specified incidence structures. They distinguish permission, arrival order and a persistent record. They do not provide an origin of physical time, a Born probability or a quantum particle.
