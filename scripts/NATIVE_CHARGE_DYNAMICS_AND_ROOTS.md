# Can the same integer be read and conserved?

The folded sixteen-site carrier and a retained two-step mark give five
binary port records, divided into blocks of three and two. There are now
two different computations on this *same* setup. One counts concordant
minus discordant pairs of records. The other constructs a reversible
collision menu without ever checking a numerical charge and solves for
all additive invariants of its completed transitions.

## What the collision law buys

Exchanging two occupied/empty ports within a block on one packet, and
exchanging two columns between two packets when both remain admissible,
leave exactly three independent additive functions: packet number and
the two block occupancy counts, here called C and W. A third, synchronized
channel starts with two blank packets and a third nonblank packet, chooses
a uniquely identifiable port by block and occupancy, and moves all three
packets along that port. Its inverse is included; all other inputs idle.
There are seven such templates, giving six distinct invariant lines once
packet number and the choice of unit/sign are set aside. One adds the
occupancy increment (6,4), leaving **−2C + 3W**. It agrees exactly with
the separately implemented pair ballot for every site in all 160 frames.
This is an algebraic cross-check on a shared carrier, not 2,560 independent
physical observations. The grammar was designed after the ballot was known;
its menu, synchronized firing, retained mark and preparations were supplied.
Activating all seven channels leaves only packet number.

Conserving a quantity does not make its values complete descriptions of
the dynamics. Under the matching menu, the 816 unordered three-packet
states form 30 communicating classes but only 27 distinct totals. An
all-blank preparation stays blank. A deliberate two-hop preparation
opens a nontrivial 18-state class. Nor does filtering by conservation
explain the rule: a separate calculation *imposes* neutrality and varies
which hops are available. At total zero, conversions changing C and W
separately first appear with three packets for arbitrary rearrangement,
five for independently selected one-hop ports, six for a common port.

## Root geometry is conditional on what is measured

Give the five signed germ flags equal Euclidean weight. A search over
chords for actual reflections permuting every endpoint finds exactly
40 signed roots of type D5; these are the short chords, **not** the
graph edges. The finite reflection group has 1,920 elements. Fixing the
blank leaves 20 roots of type A4. Fixing the ballot leaves eight of
type A2 + A1 and twelve finite permutations. Using pairwise products of
flags instead, with their own equal-weight metric, gives no reflecting
chords on the very same graph. Continuous gauge fields do not follow
from finite permutations. One Abelian direction remains if one chooses
the rank-four span; two remain in the full rank-five space.

A string-motivated *mathematical* comparison checks a different extra
requirement, a positive even unimodular ambient lattice. The D5 lattice
cannot be enlarged to such a lattice at unchanged rank; an explicit E8
completion reaches the minimal rank eight. Its 240 roots project to
40 D5 roots, 12 zeros, 60 vector weights with multiplicity included,
and 64 spinor weights of **each** chirality (four copies of each
sixteen). A tested rank-sixteen alternative has no spinor roots in the
chosen projection. No compactification, generations or string dynamics
has been inferred. See [Viazovska's lattice construction](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.04246)
and the [original heterotic proposal](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.54.502).

## Normalizations versus measurements

Assign Y = ballot / 6, and half the signed difference of the two small
port bits to T3. The equal-weight traces are 10/3 for Y², 2 for T3² and
zero for Y T3. The traces normalize generators, not coupling strengths.
Only with a standard common normalized kinetic coupling does the tree
angle give sin²θ = 3/8. Equating this directly to the Z-pole effective
angle predicts an electron asymmetry of −4/5, opposite in sign to
[SLD's +0.15138 ± 0.00216](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-ex/0004026).
[CMS measured 0.23152 ± 0.00031](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07622)
for the effective leptonic angle with independent proton collision data.
That comparison rejects the direct pole identification, **not** a
high-energy common coupling. With externally supplied standard fields,
representations, normalization and [one-loop running](https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.5868),
a complete Weyl family shifts each of the three beta coefficients by
4/3: their differences cannot count complete generations or pick the
conjugate spectrum.

The sixteen sites are not sixteen established particles. In particular
a neutral singlet in an assigned multiplet is not an experimental
discovery. Masses, spin-statistics, generations, the gauge principle,
couplings and single-event readout are not obtained from this milestone.
