# Primitive search: a common stock and a whole event

**Working verdict.** Keep signed composition with a reusable two-port
exchange as a candidate. It gives a reason for the quadratic **relative
weights** and a separate finite way to route a single selected event.
It has not explained which event nature selects, that space or matter is
made of these records, or why this carrier should describe our universe.
The results below are conditional mathematics, executable fixtures and
separately identified published observations, not new formal-proof claims.

### Strongest chain, with its assumptions exposed

1. Supply signed additive tokens, two addressable ports, a full-state
   two-step exchange up to a positive integer gain, and a nonnegative
   stock additive between the ports. At internal rank two, **one detectable
   same-port return with the bypass physically shuttered** rules out a
   nonscalar action on equal inputs. Mere exchange of observed port labels
   is insufficient. [Shutter argument and controls](TRANSPARENCY_FROM_A_SHUTTER.md).
2. At the smallest allowed integer scale, even two initially unrelated
   port responses and an unknown common gain must agree. The weight of
   nonzero x is proportional to the number of lattice cells modulo the
   integer span of x and its quarter-turn Jx. In the selected square basis
   this is m²+n². A finite inverse computation has 625 equations on 184
   separate values and rank 183; only the scale is free on those states.
   [Port selection](PORT_STOCK_ORIGIN.md). If the *named* internal rails
   must also count separately, only the two orientations of the integer
   mixer remain. [Independent raw-rail check](SEPARABLE_STOCK_SELECTION.md).
3. [Whole-event routing](ROUTED_EVENT_CANDIDATE.md) uses a signed cloud,
   two addressable marks and reversible matchings. Its ranked and expanded
   implementations agree on 90,000 individual routes. Simple standalone
   coset addresses do not pass both translation-equivariance controls.
   A persistent deterministic counter per incoming address reproduces
   ensemble frequencies, but has distinctive temporal correlations.
   [Timing, absorber, loss and photonic comparisons](EVENT_STREAM_AND_DETECTORS.md).

### Is a lattice a premise or a conclusion?

The [carrier-resolution probe](probe_carrier_resolution.py) tests both
directions without using either to justify the premises of the other.
Give an additive visible subgroup of a real two-dimensional inner-product
plane, stable under a genuine isometric quarter-turn. If the infimum m
of nonzero squared lengths is positive, choose v with W(v)<2m. Rounding
in the orthogonal equal-length basis v,Jv would leave a group element
of weight at most W(v)/2<m; it must vanish. The group is thus exactly
the integer span of v,Jv. If m=0, such square meshes have arbitrarily
small spacing, so the group is dense. A dense group can still lack the
exact half of a named unit. Invisible zero-weight components survive
every visible test: only the quotient by them is at issue.

This is an alternative conditional route, **not** a simultaneous derivation
of the plane, the stock floor and the lattice. Ordinary normalized data
do not certify a smallest positive stock. Integer and rational coordinates
can give identical rays after clearing one denominator across all coherent
arms and sibling outcomes. In the dyadic extension R=ℤ[i,1/2], the same
state 2 still has norm squared 4; R/2R is trivial, whereas quotienting by
the *integer* span of 2 and 2i has infinite index. These coincide with
each other only in the original lattice example, not after changing the
carrier. Resolving a primitive single cell would require a fixed,
independently calibrated stock scale, not renormalized counts at each depth.

### Fast screening without rejecting a good candidate

Run `python3 scripts/replay_primitive_probe_campaign.py --bundle fast`
for two cheap controls, `--list` for topics, or `--bundle all --json`
for the original 42 probes and the subsequent topic bundles. Nonzero exits, timeouts and launch errors are
separate from successful *execution*. A counterexample is often a
successful probe, not a failed run. This suite does not replace or
update the canonical candidate archive:
`python3 scripts/check_candidate_history.py` compares its saved baselines.
The [source inventory](../data/primitive_campaign_source_inventory.json)
also retains nine families from this campaign, including their missing
physical links. Those archive records have an unsupported physical
prediction adapter; that is neither a rejection nor a replacement for
running the corresponding probes.

A [second immutable source inventory](../data/primitive_operational_routes_inventory.json)
retains eight later proposals, including the unsigned pair, cycle-carrier,
three-family mixing, detector-duplication and independent-source routes.
For an actual six-entry unsigned packet table there is now also an
[executable candidate input](../data/unsigned_pair_packet_controls.json).
The source-only record and the executable finite contract remain distinct.

The [error-certificate and experiment-design probe](probe_stock_error_certificates.py)
translates simultaneous residual bounds into bounds on four specific
stock contrasts, with explicit rational coefficients available via
`--show-rows`. It also changes one untested weight without changing
any of the 625 measured equations. No claim about a whole carrier
should be promoted from that finite grid.

[When can repeated whole events count as independent draws?](OPERATIONAL_SAMPLING.md)
records an ordered-gap criterion, collision and bias controls, and a sharp
two-state competitor. The archived probes distinguish a visible sequence
of records from an assumed microscopic source of randomness.
The [record-order controls](DETECTOR_ORDER_CONTROLS.md) separate unobservable
tag histories, stored streams of random digits, and the limited power of a
fixed order test on the accepted Delft event-ready Bell trials. The optional
empirical replay explicitly reports untested when the pinned data are absent.

The [native frame and detector controls](NATIVE_FRAME_RULES.md) separate
three different experiments: projective measurements on three lossless
ports, the addition of one initially blank port, and arbitrary complete
multi-output tests. The last admits a conditional density-matrix law and a
finite five-rate check; none of the three supplies an individual click.

[Autonomous control and finite memory](AUTONOMOUS_PROGRAM_AND_MEMORY.md)
distinguishes an additive pilot from a real programmable interaction, and
tests homogeneous finite-state responses at successively longer circuit
depths. A finite list of depths never rules out arbitrarily large clocks.

[Unsigned motifs and graph cycles](UNCOLORED_CYCLE_PRIMITIVES.md)
produce possible phase carriers, but isolated darkness is not cancellation
in every context. The [unsigned two-mark junction](UNSIGNED_PAIR_JUNCTION.md)
independently finds the two mixer orientations among 46,656 packet rules.
An all-schedule pairing procedure implements them under a finite collision
contract. The whole-event readout obtained for raw counts still does not
give squared deviations from a positive background.

One cheap **proposed comparison**, not a performed experiment: inject a
fixed state, repeat the same two-mixer word t times, and read one port.
At t=2 the ideal model gives 1/8, separated from every deterministic
fraction with at most four equally weighted atoms by at least 1/8. With
an observed conditional rate of exactly 1/8, no false clicks, and otherwise
adversarial deletion, a certified efficiency strictly greater than 6/7 is
needed for that exclusion. Unequal atom weights are a different hypothesis.

For three fixed atoms with arbitrary weights and deterministic binary
readout, the three probabilities must lie on one of the triangles spanned
by cube vertices. Among 220 triples with t≤12, the largest exact
noiseless separation is attained by five triples. The cheapest in maximum
depth, then in total gate count, is **(1,3,12)**: the competitor misses
at least one ideal rate by 864387/16777216 (about 5.15 percentage points).
A three-atom distribution attains this boundary; it is not a fitted
laboratory parameter.
With a *hypothetical*, independently bounded operator error of 1/500 per
unitary gate, the cheapest of the five best certified triples changes to
**(1,3,4)** and the remaining bound is 427/32000. The equivalent
certificates at (3,9,12) and (3,4,9), respectively, require more gates.
These bounds are not statistical confidence
intervals. Fresh stochastic responses, variable initial mixtures or loss
outside its calibrated interval must not be rejected using this test.

For comparison with the quaternion alternative, [Procopio et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/ncomms15044)
compared two optical elements traversed in opposite orders and reported
a model-dependent effective phase bound of 0.03°. This is evidence about
those implemented operations and their calibration; it does not establish
that all possible phases in nature commute. No empirical parameters were
fit in these probes, and no public theory or manifest status changed.
