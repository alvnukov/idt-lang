# Comparing two different pairings of the same six records

A single four-leg junction has one natural pairing by complementary edges.
Here we **add** neighboring devices with different pairings of the same six
persistent labels. This is extra structure: it is not generated by renaming
the legs of one tetrahedron. There are 15 pairings, with 60 undirected links
between pairings sharing no pair. An active loop of devices is not the same
as rewriting one unchanged system in several charts.

| Rule on a disjoint link M→N | Outcome |
| --- | --- |
| [Reassign labels, preserving opposite marks](probe_pairing_natural_transport.py) | Exactly two reversible pair-local permutation rules respect every common relabeling. One gives a 24-element tetrahedral loop group; the other also supplies a central sign on the odd directions and gives 48. If M,N share one pair there is **no** equivariant deterministic permutation intertwiner. A random choice is a different model and can erase information. |
| [Average with the next pairing](probe_matching_transport.py) | On the two centered even directions the amplitude is multiplied by one half times a signed permutation. The normalized loop group has 12 elements. On the three odd directions **no nonempty changing path** is a nonzero scalar isometry: a common pair loses rank; disjoint links have a mod-two invariant incompatible with equal attenuation. Postselection here is informative, not a free reversible change of frame. |
| [Require a repeatable full-state echo](probe_matching_echo_selector.py) | Supply real linearity, uniform transparency, relabeling equivariance, U M=N U and U²=NM using the *same* device twice. There are four roots. Darkness between the two three-cycle orbits plus nonzero return to the input address select **(2I+2NM−(NM)²)/3**. All four are orthogonal as an algebraic consequence; no metric was used to select the root. |

The selected root also happens to be the polar, or shortest-turn, choice.
It is crucial that the two other requirements are checked independently:
the longer turn (NM)² is an equally valid root but has zero same-address
return. Two explicit loops of the selected roots give rotations with trace
115/81 about different axes. Their closure contains SO(3) on the three odd
coordinates. This is internal real rotation in an explicitly arranged
network, **not** local color gauge dynamics or measured spacetime curvature.

### Can positive raw records carry the rotation?

[Five positive descendants](probe_matching_transport_cost.py) per input
implement gain three on the odd *differences*. Nine implement gain three
on all five centered directions using a uniform background. If only one
conserved classical record is routed with fixed probabilities, sharp
uniform contrast gains are respectively **3/5** and **1/3**. The general
centered upper bound permits an arbitrary input-independent bias. The
integer construction uses growing stock; normalizing by its actual raw
population attenuates the signal. These effects cannot be relabeled as
lossless single-particle interference.

### A dense rotation group does not by itself select the square law

The [ternary-stock test](probe_ternary_stock_ambiguity.py) considers an
even nonnegative component stock f with f(0)=0, added over three independent
components. All triples with rational coordinates in Z[1/3] must be
preparable, and the selected transfer must preserve that stock. The
resulting possibilities are **f(x)=A x²+B ε(x)**, A,B≥0, where ε is the
parity of the numerator at an odd power-of-three denominator. The second
term survives every selected link even though the real rotations are dense.
It is invisible to an argument that assumes continuity at the outset.

There is a cheap discriminator: f(6)f(1)−f(3)f(2)=32AB. A verified common
tripling gain on these preparations and f(2)>0 remove the parity term
without fitting a quadratic coefficient. Finite uncertainty can leave this
test inconclusive. Strict integer-valued stock at arbitrarily fine ternary
resolution instead excludes nonzero A. Without independent additivity,
the fourth power of the vector length is a separate invariant counterexample.
None of these stock statements specifies which individual detector clicks.
