# Is the ternary mark a particle moving on the binary substrate?

The [successful open-path encoding](WHEN_UNCLOCKED_CONTACTS_OVERLAP.md) fixes which clean words can reach each other. It does **not** preserve the path traveled by a visible mark, or even the network relation when an open chain is closed. These four holdouts keep the six contact triples fixed.

**Next event.** Stop at the first *different* globally clean observation, without inspecting or resetting intermediate states. Already at three sites the two path orientations allow 10 and 16 extra directed next observations, respectively, relative to one native ternary move. On BAB, for example, `Tbb → aTa` takes three raw flips with no clean intermediate state. Moreover a clean `Taa` can reach clean `aaT` without the middle site's two rails ever taking their T code. This is a support statement, with no rates assumed. [First-observation and detector probes](probe_staggered_next_event.py)

**What can a local detector tell?** Start a B-first chain either with one prepared T on the second site or with all a. Offer the same BA contact: it stutters in the first run and emits a T plus two changed helper bits in the other. From that instant the raw states differ *only* at the original helper pair. Use the same onward contacts without touching that pair. Every bit outside the source site and the two helpers has the same entire history in both runs, even at equal offered-contact indices. A detector reading that history may register the passage; it cannot certify which preparation produced it. The final state of the compensated run is not globally clean. Monitoring the source or the original helper distinguishes the two preparations. No frequencies or false-count rates have been inferred.

**What does move?** [Signed charge and positive bookkeeping](probe_staggered_raw_charge.py) assign weights (1,0) to A's rails, (1,−1) to B's and (0,−1) to the BA helpers. Every changing triple contains one +1, one −1 and one zero. One unit of signed charge transfers between the two weighted slots, including between cells when the slots differ in location. At a clean observation its total is exactly the number of T's. Replacing each negative term by the corresponding hole gives nonnegative occupancies moving against a filled background. This background matters: separate an emitted T arbitrarily far from its compensating helper. Any additive observable made of bounded-range terms sees the separated defects independently. With zero vacuum count, conservation forces an isolated compensating defect to contribute −1; repeated distant pairs require a growing offset if the total must be nonnegative on **all** raw states. This statement concerns this encoding, its vacuum and locality assumptions, not a physical charge or a photon.

**Can the rails themselves be relative?** The [pure equality/difference implementation](probe_staggered_relational_sector.py) identifies globally complemented configurations. Between clean words each carrying exactly one T, it preserves the successful open-path reachability without an absolute reference bit. Without the promised sector a single BA contact connects the relational images of native `ab` and `ba`, which cannot move. An inert comparison bit restores the missing distinction. None of this supplies an intrinsic preparation, clean-cut herald, or equality of microscopic trajectories.

**Change only the network.** Close the chain or connect it at a fork; do not change the law. On even rings of 4, 6 and 8 sites the [topology test](probe_staggered_topology.py) finds 21, 357 and 3,615 extra unordered clean pairs, with none missing. On the three-leaf and double junction every one of the 16 and 64 assignments tested also has extras. On a bipartite tree with a branch, two arms of the branch must share their bond type; each of the four cases has a local spurious path surviving padding by exterior T's. This rules out this exact wiring prescription as a topology-independent implementation. It does not rule out the two-state contact with different wiring, nor does it derive spatial dimensionality.

These are finite gates and explicit arguments with stated premises. They leave the microscopic law, observation, experiment-to-model dictionary and actual quantum dynamics open.
