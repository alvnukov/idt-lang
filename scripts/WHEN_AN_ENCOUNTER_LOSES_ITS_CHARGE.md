# When an encounter loses its charge

Can the origin used by the five-flag encounter rule be supplied by its own participants? On the 16-record folded carrier a multiset, unlike an ordered list, admits a simple reversible exchange. Start with three copies of a record and one neighboring record of the allowed difference. Leave one copy as the reference; transform the other three by the relative rule. There is no choice of *which* equal copy on a multiset. The product has a recognizable inverse. The supplied split of five flag directions into groups of three and two is still an input.

`probe_anonymous_reference_collision.py` enumerates all 3,876 unordered quartets: 96 inverse pairs move, 3,684 quartets are idle. There is no corresponding deterministic, slot-equivariant choice of the one original participant that stayed behind: the three identical arrivals have a permutation symmetry that the assigned roles would break. A reversible map of occupations need not provide packet identities.

For the closed quartet the sum of a field over the six unordered pairs is conserved for a ten-dimensional space of pair fields. Adding the earlier two closed relative encounter families cuts this to two dimensions: constants and the signed ballot with coefficients −2 and +3 on the two flag groups. This constraint uses the specified families, not just the anonymous move. These are dimensionless finite charges, not electromagnetic charges.

**Isolation matters.** `(0,0,0,9)` can become `(0,23,30,30)`. The internal pair sum stays at 3. Leave one record `3` untouched nearby and count all ten pairs: the sum changes from −8 to 4. Another failure occurs even if one tries to recover isolation from the records themselves. In `probe_local_cluster_charge_descent.py`, an eight-record system joins two unambiguous endpoint decompositions by two allowed moves. Summing the values of complete quartets (including an idle quartet) gives 6 at the start and 18 at the end. Partitioning into active quartets only does not settle the ambiguity in the middle. The probe enumerates the reachable components at sizes five through eight and retains distinct escape routes.

A chamber with capacity four and a boundary preventing transfer preserves the internal sum. The chamber, not a permanent name for each packet, can supply the missing isolation. A leak spoils the control. This keeps the local exchange as a candidate and excludes these two unrestricted global-charge readouts; it does not exclude nonadditive laws, dynamically produced chambers, or other fields.
