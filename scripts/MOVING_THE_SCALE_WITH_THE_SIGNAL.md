# Moving the scale with the signal

The [one-stock contact](SIGNED_MARKS_ARE_NOT_CLICKS.md) fails on overlapping junctions because part of its state remains behind at the first junction. Could a mark instead carry its place in a local hierarchy?

Take an incoming signed mark on one of three ports. Return one opposite mark there and send two same-sign marks to each other port, all one refinement level deeper. On each wire let opposites cancel and let three equal marks at one level replace one mark one level shallower. The update uses only integers; the comparator interprets a mark at level k as 3⁻ᵏ. The [moving-scale probe](probe_mobile_refinement_field.py) recovers the exact signed three-port reflection on overlapping contacts. This is reversible on the normalized records, **not** on discarded branch histories. The hierarchy can extend without bound: two overlapping contacts repeated 48 times require level 96 for the exact unit input, but only 273 nonzero balanced digits are stored across four wires at that endpoint. This removes the stationary-stock obstruction at the cost of a genuine new resource.

In the restricted class of permutation-symmetric one-step integer substitutions with the same refinement factor m at every output, the coefficients are not free. If one supplied uniform signed level is preserved and repeating a contact acts as an echo after coarsening, the nonidentity rule returns 2m/d−m to the incoming port and sends 2m/d to each other one. The smallest possible m is d/gcd(d,2). Without a one-level calibration, however, a two-step echo does not by itself identify one parent with three children. An observable that doubles the gain at odd depths passes the raw echo and disagrees on the three-contact circuit. Annotations, signed flux, uniformity, the comparator and unbounded storage are not derived by this calculation.

**Do trees supply the level relation?** In an unordered ternary tree, a parent is the sum of *its own* three children. At depth two only one of the 84 distinct triples cancels a fixed parent of leaves. A device that ignores descent and only counts the absolute level accepts all 84 triples. The [tree probe](probe_anonymous_refinement_relations.py) separates a record invariant under relabeling from a quotient that declares relabeled leaves interchangeable. The first gives the fixed whole-root sum; the latter gives the transferable ternary scale. Two trees with separately retained origins still have independent normalizations until one actual cross-source comparison joins them.

**Do these tests force rational arithmetic?** Not from a finite list of nulls. In an arithmetic modulo a prime other than three, division by three exists and the same local reflector conserves the signed sum and its algebraic square. Such a square in a finite field is not a positive probability. The [null-panel search](probe_modular_contact_nulls.py) compares just one port after fixed repeated overlaps. At four commands its rational level is −40/81 while arithmetic modulo five gives zero. For the 24 design primes through 97 (excluding three), nine bright-port tests after 19,24,26,29,30,32,35,40,43 two-contact blocks distinguish *each from the rational comparator*; they do not necessarily distinguish every pair from one another. Nine is minimal within the first sufficient 43-block prefix for that port and the imposed minimum rational amplitude 1/4. Untrained primes 101 and 127 match all nine null flags. The larger prime 1009 happens to fail one. For any finite rational design, primes dividing none of its nonzero numerators or denominators preserve all its zero/nonzero answers.

To replay, use the `ternary_network_nulls` checker with the [39 fixed synthetic hypotheses](../data/ternary_network_null_controls.json). It accepts observed dark/bright flags without requiring an amplitude or probability. It compares a moving hierarchy, stationary stocks, a finite-field rule and an idle wire. The archive contains both possible short outcomes, echoes that cannot discriminate, the full nine-read design and two surviving alternatives. A mismatch only rejects the specified model on the specified comparator. No physical measurement of those nulls has been made here.
