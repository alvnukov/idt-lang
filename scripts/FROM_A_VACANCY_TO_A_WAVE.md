# From a vacancy to a wave: what one exchange can and cannot do

Start without a positive/negative mark: choose an edge and exchange its endpoint bits, so 10 and 01 trade places. Give the graph a source and sinks. In a second run with every occupation and reservoir complemented, the same edge selections reverse every real transfer. At a junction, incoming transfers minus all outgoing transfers equal the change in the local stored bit. This equality holds **on each run**, without a probability, clock distribution or fractional unit. It is an origin for a relative sign and the composition of *currents*. It is not superposition of two physical histories.

**A distal shutter distinguishes three interpretations of the same apparent thirds.** A filled source feeds three intermediate sites, each with three empty terminal contacts. Retain only one of the first site's three exits. With independent uniform proposals of active edges, that arm's share of terminal transfers changes from 1/3 to **1/4**. A forward selector that keeps choosing one of the three arms returns **1/3**, and equal weighting of the seven remaining terminals gives **1/7**. For k open exits the site fills at unit rate and empties at rate k, hence its mean flux is k/(1+k) in equal per-edge time units. The [exact check](probe_vacancy_cut_flux.py) exhausts 96 and 80 state/edge transitions and separately solves the stationary chain. Two deterministic schedules can propose every edge equally often and give different shares. Stationarity, independently chosen edges and the reservoir protocol are additional to the exchange rule. Related primary models are the [open symmetric exclusion process](https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/0105110) and [deterministic rotor approximations to Markov hitting frequencies](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.4507); neither measures this shutter.

**A static false positive for wave scattering.** At a star, let uᵢ be the mean input occupancy and h the initial hub mean. The native mean outward change on port i is (h−uᵢ)/d. At stationarity h is the mean of the uᵢ. A calibrated meter that adds c times this change to uᵢ gives the exact wave reflection 2 mean(u)−uᵢ when c=2d. That gain is selected by demanding a nonidentity static echo within this meter family; the bit sent to the port is *not* this value. In a three-port common drive alternating between 1/4 and 3/4 occupancy, the fixed meter reads 5/4 and −1/4, whereas the ideal reflector preserves the incident common signal. A different instrument using the *next* hub plus external arithmetic can still display the wave table. A [two-junction raw cascade](probe_static_current_reflection.py) has a port which remains dark when the composed ideal reflections give a signed field 8/9. The readout interface matters as much as the local rule.

**What adding inertia changes.** Replace the occupancy law by actual displacements, equal springs along discrete arms, masses m, and a junction of mass μm. These are new resources. Now the outgoing wave travels on the arms; its signed deviation is not reconstructed by subtracting a calibrated current. All junction masses in this comparison yield the same long-wavelength star reflection. At quarter-wave advance per link on a three-arm star, equal *site* masses give energy fractions (1/5,2/5,2/5) for one incident arm; half a bead's mass from each incident *edge* gives (1/9,4/9,4/9). Only μ=d/2 preserves the equal-star matrix throughout the propagating band in this spring model. [Avdonin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.09274) target the same mass sum in a discrete-time approximation to continuum metric graphs, not as an experimental selection of fundamental mass.

An independent discriminator is to excite the junction itself. For d=3 and μ=1, a normalizable mode alternates as (−1/2)ⁿ down an arm and oscillates above the arm's band. For μ=3/2 it is absent. A vacancy density perturbation can have the **same snapshot** but decays without reversing sign under equal-rate exchanges. The [610-case scattering and local-mode check](probe_edge_inertia_scattering.py) keeps these preparations separate. A measured sign reversal after the drive is removed would be more informative than matching one stationary shape. Energy fractions in this model are not probabilities of absorption of one quantum.
