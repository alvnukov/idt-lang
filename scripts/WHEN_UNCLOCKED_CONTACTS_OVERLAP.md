# What survives when no one orders the contacts?

**A two-valued substrate can reproduce the allowed connections between ternary words on an open path without a clock or resets.** The successful construction needs alternating site codes and different contact supports on alternate bonds. An isolated working gate, even one with its helper restored, does not guarantee that a network of such gates works.

The physical move E is just `000 ↔ 111` on any enabled three-site contact. The comparison law on neighboring *encoded* sites is `aa ↔ bb; Ta ↔ aT`. We compare **all pairs of clean endpoints reachable by any sequence**, including sequences passing through non-code states. A clean endpoint means every site's two-bit value is in its code and every private helper is back at its initial value. The bits, supports, and observation convention stay fixed during a run.

## The positive example, in six triples

Write the codes in order (T,a,b). One site type A uses (1,0,2), its neighbor B uses (1,0,3); integer bit 0 is the first rail. Number an edge's left rails 0,1 and its right rails 2,3. On AB use triples **013, 123** and no active helpers. On BA add two private bits, numbered 4,5, initially zero; use **014, 045, 235, 245**. Never reset a bit. At length six, both choices of starting type reproduce all 6,660 native unordered connected pairs and add none.

There is an algebraic reason to take this result beyond the small-size search. Assign native words values in the free product of the commuting pair (an involution a and an infinite-order T) with a second involution b. In the native word calculus the double letter c=aa=bb moves past any letter. Extract these pairs, commute a past T, and reduce again after neighboring b's cancel. The remaining alternating blocks have a unique free-product normal form; the original length fixes how many double letters were removed. Thus equality of the group value at fixed length characterizes native connectivity on an open path.

The witness gives values also to the dirty rail and helper states. Multiplying their factors in path order, with the helper factor inserted on each BA bond, preserves the group value on **all** local changing links (4 bare AB links and 32 BA links). Conversely, four explicit local raw paths implement the two native exchanges in both orientations with restored helpers. Neighbors need not act during those paths. This is an algebraic argument with finite executable local certificates and independently checkable full-path controls. It is **not** a Lean proof artifact or evidence that nature uses these rails.

The construction removes the prescribed order, not the supplied path, alternating roles, incidence, stock, or definition of a clean observation. Equality of reachable endpoints does not imply equality of first observations, probabilities, time intervals, rates or particle trajectories. In particular, no scheduler probabilities were used for the positive connectivity result.
