# When can a four-place rule generate thirds?

The three-port reflection sends a vector x to S3 x = (2/3) sum(x) (1,1,1) − x. Finding its entries in a table does not tell us what is moving, how to join two contacts, or how a detector selects an event. Five small, replayable probes separate these questions.

**An unsigned construction.** Put marks on exactly two of four ports. Replace them by the complementary pair and feed the fourth output back as the next fourth input. That bit alternates. Across 2k active firings the exterior input counts m have sum 3k and each lies between 0 and 2k. Each admissible integer count vector is reachable; the output counts are exactly 2k (1,1,1) − m = S3 m. Nothing on a single tick was negative or fractional. Over a fixed number of firings, differences have sum zero, so only their reversal is visible. Comparing the preparations (1,1,1) in two firings and (4,1,1) in four gives an input difference (3,0,0) and an output difference (−1,2,2). This is subtraction of two records with different activity, not a negative mark. [Raw window and reachability probe](probe_raw_pair_window_reflection.py).

**Why the next contact matters.** A neighboring junction can receive equal total counts as the sequences 100,011 and 000,111. The former is legal at a suitable mirror phase; the latter cannot fire immediately at either phase. With one firing available per tick and no backpressure, a source can leave at least 2n unconsumed marks halfway through a balanced 4n-tick run. This counts marks, not bits of memory. A separate reversible recoding test fixes the duration, phases and wire counts. For its balanced 2k-tick family it needs at least C(2k,k) distinguishable side states; saving the original spectator trace attains the bound. For k=1,2,3 the complete arrival sets have 6, 90 and 1,860 words, and require 2, 6 and 20 side states. A clock, extra wire or variable duration can carry that information instead. The inverse here is offline; a local physical router has not been constructed. [Exact counting and inverse](probe_reversible_pair_retiming.py).

**Two different ways of supplying what the counts omit.** If a fourth return carries a signed coherent signal with a one-tick delay, a dyadic four-port contact has S3 as its constant-drive response. An isolated pulse has the same *integrated signed signal* as the instantaneous reflection, but its *time-resolved energy* integrates to (1/3,1/3,1/3) rather than (1/9,4/9,4/9) for a launch at the first port. A static solution on a tree is not a guarantee that every internal state relaxes. [Causal feedback and static trees](probe_feedback_ternary_from_pairs.py). Alternatively, a coordinate-free comparison of directed edges can support additive displacements under supplied incidence and interchange conditions. But forward fourth-corner completions from rational labels 0 and 1 stay integral; they cannot build the required thirds. Finite arithmetic and a nontransitive rival give different outcomes. [Relation and arithmetic controls](probe_relational_balance_scatter.py).

**A prospective distinguishing measurement, not observed data.** Launch isolated pulses separately at ports 0 and 1; measure energies at ports 0 and 1. The ratio of the product of the two diagonal readings to the product of the other two is 1/16 for direct reflection and 1 for the delayed loop. Positive separable source and detector gains cancel; a detector's time window must be the same for the two launches and yield nonzero readings. Under independent residual relative errors of at most ε per reading, the bands remain disjoint for ε < 1/3. Unknown additive background or input-dependent detection efficiency can invalidate the contrast. Coherently summing amplitudes before squaring is a different measurement. [Gain, timing and counterfeit controls](probe_feedback_pulse_gain_control.py).

The new lead is an origin for a restricted count transformation. None of these alternatives derives unconditioned signal composition, the Born rule, detector actualization or a Standard Model particle.
