# What local particle labels cannot decide

**A charge measurement and a group-center measurement ask different questions.** On the sixteen even five-flag records let C,W be the occupation counts in the marked 3+2 groups. The integer q=−2C+3W obeys q≡C (mod 3) and q≡W (mod 2). Assign the usual color and weak center characters to those occupations and a primitive period to the Abelian angle. A combined sixfold phase then acts trivially on all the local labels, so it acts trivially on any product and dual of them. This is not a new derivation of the center: see the earlier [five-slot probe](probe_five_slot_charge.py). It also does **not** single out the global group.

The [global-options probe](probe_flag_center_blindness.py) compares retaining the full product gauge group and quotienting it by a central subgroup of order 2, 3 or 6. In a 36-class electric-residue menu those choices admit 36, 18, 12 or 6 classes, respectively, but all include the same sixteen local records. Two *hypothetical genuine external singlet lines* of charges q=2,3 have allowed pairs (yes,yes), (yes,no), (no,yes), (no,no). Absence of a new particle cannot establish that a mathematical line is forbidden. Purely magnetic line options additionally assume the stated quantization and theta choice. This distinction and sign conventions follow [Tong, §3](https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP07(2017)104), not a measurement of a global group. On the same even records, q′=−5C+6W has identical residues modulo six and signs, including q′=1 on the mixed mark, but changes by −6 in a collision preserving q. A residue match is weaker than the signed dynamical conservation test.

**Spin and exchange.** In the [two-packet test](probe_pair_cup_exchange.py) a local preparation creates a pair of internal modes; a matched apparatus can absorb the pair back to the original channel. A finite binary rotation group has a two-component complex mode and a special central element acting as minus identity. Taking that element as a *proxy* for a full spatial turn is an extra input. The single doublet has one invariant pair state up to scale, antisymmetric under internal-index interchange. Yet the sign η carried by physically exchanging the two packets is still independent. With an interferometric reference a putative exchange through this apparatus measures η times the parity of the prepared pair; a return intensity by itself loses the sign altogether.

Adding two passive flavor labels leaves the central action unchanged but permits symmetric as well as antisymmetric pair preparations. The framed two-packet algebra independently admits all four assignments of signs to a turn and an exchange. A nonzero local pair source in the same complete orbital that is unchanged by physically interchanging its outputs imposes an additional relation; it is not supplied by simply declaring the source neutral. This leaves spin–statistics and the Pauli principle as independent physical obligations for a candidate. It does not question those effects in the established theory and it does not derive them from a sixteen-record carrier.
