# When is a square a physical count rather than a fitted readout?

Replay: `python3 -m unittest tests.test_phase_color_candidates`. These
three finite probes distinguish sampling, composition, and the stock of
states on which a claimed reversible device actually works.

### Blind samples or access to a common mechanism?

Suppose an unknown source returns independent signs with mean μ=2p−1.
Can a device emit **one nonnegative click** with probability μ²? At
p=1/2 every finite sign transcript has positive probability. A positive
apparatus whose click is decided on a finite transcript cannot be dark
there and light somewhere else, even with almost surely finite stopping.
Two independent signs have product of expectation μ², but their product
can be −1. A signed estimate is not a physical click.

The [positive readout probe](probe_positive_square_readout.py) checks the
sharp fixed-budget alternative. With n≥2 samples and v≥0 the click law
c+vμ² is possible exactly when c+v≤1 and c≥v/d, with d=n for odd n and
d=n−1 for even n. Condition on observing k plus signs: the unique
symmetrized coefficient is c+v((2k−n)²−n)/(n(n−1)). These coefficients
must themselves be probabilities. The probe checks positivity and the
polynomial independently; endpoint-only or biased rules do not count as
passing the whole curve.

Change the *joint* experiment. On an ordered list of N sites let m be
positive in a nested family of cuts; compare a site and its reflected
partner. Exactly |2m−N| sites return an equal pair. Coincidence between
two independently sampled such comparisons has probability μ² without
a negative click. A shuffled balanced cut, or reusing the same comparison
twice, breaks the result. Three balanced partitions can each be paired
with either of the others without any common pairing for all three.
This mechanism supplies addressable correlated queries and a common
cut structure; ordinary mixing of two preparations is linear in their
mixing probability, not the square of that mean.

### What does composition of phase colors select?

In the [phase-color probe](probe_composed_phase_colors.py) cancelling
signed colors gives integer coordinates and N(x)=Σxᵢ² is the chosen
common raw weight. Supply a unit, bilinear serial composition, orthogonal
unit colors with signed-axis products, and demand N(xy)=N(x)N(y) at the
same exposure. With a second color a, N((1+a)(1−a))=4 selects a²=−1 from
the four signed possibilities. The additive lattice generated by these
two axes is then the Gaussian integers, not all of the complex numbers.

With a third orthogonal unit b, also test (1+b)(1−b) and (a+b)(a−b).
For *commuting* scalar products the three output weights are squared
distances of 1,a²,b²; their sum equals 9−N(1+a²+b²)≤9, not the required
12. For a separate four-axis XOR multiplication catalog only two of
512 sign tables pass all 100 unit/two-axis composition pairs. They are
the two orientations of quaternion multiplication, both noncommuting.
Each distinct pair of the three masks still admits commuting survivors.
Measuring each order separately cannot distinguish ab from ba by norm:
both have norm one. Coherent comparison with a common reference is a
stronger operation and must be justified separately.

The existing [Lean quadratic selection](../Proofs/QMClosure/GaussianIntegerQuadraticSelection.lean)
starts with Gaussian integers. The finite experiment here compares
different candidate products; it does not establish that nature supplies
these premises. A nonorthogonal integer lattice admits the Eisenstein
norm instead. Modulo two signs collapse; a norm that ignores some
coordinates cannot count the dimension of an unobservable sector.

### An exactly lossless but limited reversible candidate

A [two-mark shell](probe_two_mark_shell.py) uses precisely two signed
marks on distinct rails among four. Cyclically shift the rails; if the
signs are opposite, negate both, and if they agree, move them to the two
free rails without changing their sign. All 24 records are permuted,
the square swaps the two port pairs, and selecting one mark uniformly
always gives one detector event. The native rule never evaluates a square.
An independent calculation identifies its action with a scaled signed
Hadamard matrix on this shell. Quarter turns within ports are supplied.
There are six resulting classes after identifying a common quarter phase;
this comparison covers these finite states and controls only.

An extra overlapping mixer on three ports produces the target weights
(1/2,1/4,1/4), impossible for a uniform choice of one mark of **one**
two-mark record. A mixture of records does give those marginals, so the
obstruction is not to general finite classical models. The distinction
is between a single-record reusable operation and a fresh preparation.
