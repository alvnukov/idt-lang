# Can the two signed phase directions come from something simpler?

Two different candidates allow a fourfold turn without entering a signed
two-coordinate table by hand. They do not produce the same mechanics.

**Unsigned motifs.** Start with copies of one mark, commutative grouping,
and cyclic relabeling. On a four-cycle, declaring a mark plus its opposite
equivalent to vacuum in **every additive context** makes the orbit a cyclic
Gaussian-integer module. On a three-cycle, declaring the full triad neutral
gives the Eisenstein alternative. Further stable identifications can make
either a finite quotient; freeness, a calibrated norm and a probability
rule are not automatic. Fourfold full-orbit neutrality alone instead leaves
three integer directions. For counts a,b,c,d let z=a−c+i(b−d) and
h=a−b+c−d. The cyclically invariant nonnegative response
W=(1+h²)|z|² makes an opposite pair dark in isolation, but
adding it to one mark changes W from **2 to 10**. Thus isolated darkness
is not vacuum equivalence. [Unsigned motif probes](probe_unsigned_neutral_motifs.py).

The triangular alternative fails a specific **literal amplitude** echo on
two torsion-free scalar ports: in the symmetric and antisymmetric sectors
the two eigenvalues of a square root of a nonzero scalar times exchange
must have ratio squaring to −1. There is no such scalar in ℚ(√−3).
A population-only echo, a finite quotient, or a larger carrier evades this
test: the integer map (x,y)↦(x−y,x+y) already squares to (−2y,2x).
None of these observations rejects all substrates with three mark types.

**Uncolored graph.** Take two equal triangles sharing one vertex. An
ordinary graph automorphism permutes the four outer vertices and sends
the two oriented cycles (a,b) to (b,−a). The minus sign comes from
reversed traversal, not a negative edge weight. The full integral
cycle lattice carries a square isometry in its inherited edge metric.
Among connected simple graphs of cycle rank two with at most five
vertices, this is the smallest example and the only such five-vertex
isomorphism type. Allowing parallel edges reduces it to three vertices
and four edges. Allowing plus/minus conventions for loops on roses changes
the minimum again; those conventions must be stated.

The graph selects the **pair** of opposite quarter-turns, not a preferred
one: a reflection interchanges them. A simply drawn planar bowtie does
not give an orientation-preserving plane motion that implements this
cycle action. An automorphism preserving directed edge arrows and acting as
minus identity on all cycles after two turns could not preserve a nonzero
nonnegative directed circulation. Reversible traversal is a real extra
operation, not implicit in an arbitrary directed network.

Most importantly, a reversible path that cancels only immediate retracings
has the free group on two generators, **not** the additive group ℤ². The
paths ab a⁻¹ b⁻¹ and aa⁻¹ bb⁻¹ have equal raw length, equal edge counts
and equal net flow. Only the second reduces to the empty path. Counting
only net flow or adding a face whose boundary is the commutator removes
this difference. Applying an identical subsequent drive to both histories
can distinguish the free-path and additive models; actual route/path
records and detector sensitivity would be needed to test this.
[Graph census, path and surface controls](probe_uncolored_cycle_carrier.py).

These are lower **candidates**, not established features of spacetime.
They address the origin of a possible carrier and preserve competing
histories. The separate [unsigned junction](UNSIGNED_PAIR_JUNCTION.md)
addresses how a mixer could act and why a physical readout still matters.
