# Results: what the primitive search can distinguish

**No lowest physical primitive has been confirmed.** The positive result is a set of small, executable mechanisms that agree on one observation and disagree on another. One economical starting point is an exchange of a filled and an empty place: it gives opposite signed currents under reversal and an exact rule for combining transfers. It does **not** by itself give a propagating signed wave or the probabilities of individual particle detections. Keep it as a candidate for transport, not as an accepted foundation.

### Run the public demonstration

```sh
python3 scripts/public_demo.py
```

The [offline demo](scripts/public_demo.py) reads the bundled two-detector histogram, compares two hypotheses on one dark-port question, chooses a less costly measurement plan, computes the effect of closing branches, and runs the manifest verifier. It labels measured counts, conditional model calculations and governance checks separately. Add `--json` for a machine-readable report. It does not write candidate history.

### What the checker protects

- **Don't throw away an unresolved alternative.** Submitted hypotheses and baseline reports are retained. A source-only candidate without a prediction adapter is unsupported, not physically falsified. Historical stability means the recorded comparisons replay, not that a candidate is true.
- **Don't pay for redundant measurements.** On one submitted menu the best adaptive fresh-preparation plan costs 15 units versus 19 for the best fixed panel. A different menu gives 8 versus 8. An unspecified prediction remains possible on every branch. [Readout planning, limits and replay commands](scripts/CHOOSING_THE_NEXT_READOUT.md).
- **Don't mistake a numerical agreement for a derivation.** The weak-gravity/SPARC comparisons include frozen candidates failing held-out transfer. The public first-principles claims about action, gravity and electromagnetic couplings are not established. [Public claim sheet](PUBLIC_CLAIM_SHEET.md) · [physical scope](sections/150-physical-scope-boundary.md).

One instructive near match is the [three-family phase loop](scripts/WHAT_PARTICLE_LABELS_CANNOT_DECIDE.md). A fixed contact word produces a nonzero CP-odd invariant without changing either sector's individual spectrum. Its candidate CKM angle is numerically close to one experimental result, but the **same** word gives |Vud|²=5/8 and does not reproduce the observed mixing. The word remains in the research history as a source of phase structure, not as an accepted description of the quarks.

An unsigned alternative now gives the three-port reflection on raw count windows: mark two places out of four, swap occupied and empty, and return the fourth bit.
