# Results: what the primitive search can distinguish

**No lowest physical primitive has been confirmed.** The positive result is a set of small, executable mechanisms that agree on one observation and disagree on another. One economical starting point is an exchange of a filled and an empty place: it gives opposite signed currents under reversal and an exact rule for combining transfers. It does **not** by itself give a propagating signed wave or the probabilities of individual particle detections. Keep it as a candidate for transport, not as an accepted foundation.

### Run the public demonstration

```sh
python3 scripts/public_demo.py
```

The [offline demo](scripts/public_demo.py) reads the bundled two-detector histogram, compares two hypotheses on one dark-port question, chooses a less costly measurement plan, computes the effect of closing branches, and runs the manifest verifier. It labels measured counts, conditional model calculations and governance checks separately. Add `--json` for a machine-readable report. It does not write candidate history.

### What the checker protects

- **Don't throw away an unresolved alternative.** Submitted hypotheses and baseline reports are retained. A source-only candidate without a prediction adapter is unsupported, not physically falsified. Historical stability means the recorded comparisons replay, not that a candidate is true.
- **Don't pay for redundant measurements.** On one submitted menu the best adaptive fresh-preparation plan costs 15 units versus 19 for the best fixed panel. A different menu gives 8 versus 8. An unspecified prediction remains possible on every branch. [Readout planning, limits and replay commands](scripts/CHOOSING_THE_NEXT_READOUT.md).
- **Don't mistake a numerical agreement for a derivation.** The weak-gravity/SPARC comparisons include frozen candidates failing held-out transfer. The public first-principles claims about action, gravity and electromagnetic couplings are not established. [Public claim sheet](PUBLIC_CLAIM_SHEET.md) · [physical scope](sections/150-physical-scope-boundary.md).

To run a research group use `python3 scripts/replay_primitive_probe_campaign.py --list` and then `--bundle NAME`. To check original candidate reports without appending new observations use `python3 scripts/check_candidate_history.py`. For experimental context beyond these examples see the [finite multiphoton controls](scripts/MULTIPHOTON_EXCHANGE.md), [spectator extension tests](scripts/SPECTATOR_EXTENSION_TEST.md), and [particle-count and phase contrasts](scripts/PARTICLE_COUNTS_AND_PHASE_CONTRASTS.md). These executable research probes do not change the Lean proof boundary or the public theory version.

One instructive near match is the [three-family phase loop](scripts/WHAT_PARTICLE_LABELS_CANNOT_DECIDE.md). A fixed contact word produces a nonzero CP-odd invariant without changing either sector's individual spectrum. Its candidate CKM angle is numerically close to one experimental result, but the **same** word gives |Vud|²=5/8 and does not reproduce the observed mixing. The word remains in the research history as a source of phase structure, not as an accepted description of the quarks.

An unsigned alternative now gives the three-port reflection on raw count windows: mark two places out of four, swap occupied and empty, and return the fourth bit. Equal totals can still arrive in an order that a neighboring contact cannot accept. [Construction, reversible storage cost and a prospective pulse test](scripts/WHEN_THIRDS_ARE_ACTUALLY_BUILT.md).

Two such contacts can use a shared bit to pass a turn in a closed cycle, but this does not route an independent input. A separate pair of rules reads only coincidences among neighboring counts. They agree on which centers can act and disagree on whether a closed circuit can change its occupancy parity. [Turns, edge readings and loop-parity controls](scripts/WHO_GIVES_A_CONTACT_ITS_TURN.md).

**Measured loop check.** In the first two published 250 K orientation tables for Apamea artificial spin ice, 45 of 232 reconstructed bounded circuits change sign parity. On the fixed graph, the changed/unchanged classifications of at least 28 of 1,061 matched islands would need to be revised to fit unrestricted full-vertex flips; a certificate shows that 28 suffice. These are classified magnetic moments in composite exposures, not instantaneous observations of fundamental bits. [Original data, reconstruction and exact edit-distance check](data/ergodicity_asi/README.md).

In a different sector of the quartet rule, one doubled packet moves at exactly one contact per chosen matching. On the smallest three-context graph meeting two explicit circuit conditions, a word of three moves acts as a commuting quarter turn on differences of counts. A connected 32-site control meets the circuit conditions and fails the conclusion. [Why size, context colors and readout matter](scripts/WHEN_PACKET_ORDER_GENERATES_A_QUARTER_TURN.md).

The 250 K reconstruction was frozen before opening the next six Apamea frame pairs. Of those six, four could be read without changing the procedure: at 260, 280, 300 and 310 K, respectively 59, 58, 57 and 47 complete faces had changed sign product. The 270 and 290 K pairs failed the existing orientation guard and remain inconclusive. This is a transfer within one experiment, not independent replication. [Protocol, source tables and all six outcomes](data/ergodicity_asi_transfer/README.md).

Changing the test graph prevents a false rejection: a rule unable to relocate a pair of odd triangles on the octahedron succeeds from 784 of 1,392 eligible eight-cycle-square states. A separate sector has no eligible starts at all. [All 256 rules, finite paths and transfer controls](scripts/WHEN_A_PAIR_OF_LOOP_DEFECTS_CAN_MOVE.md).

A retrospective location check on the same Apamea data gives a stronger test of one narrower mechanism: at 250 K none of the 42 reversed classifications touches a visible fourfold vertex. Even allowing arbitrary subsets of incident edges, covering them needs at least 34 other visible sites, and 34 suffice. [All seven settings, endpoint certificates and readout limits](data/ergodicity_asi_transfer/VISIBLE_VERTEX_SUPPORT.md).

Two-site rules can recognize a completed four-site change. On a fixed quartet, 5,448 of 6,144 four-symbol pair tables can reach the right bits, but only eight have a positive current-configuration signal that stays sound on every visit. A different rule uses a bond between two dimers to identify its own four participants inside a larger system. [Constructive rules, smaller alphabets, false signals and the clean-output limitation](scripts/WHEN_TWO_CONTACTS_CAN_NAME_FOUR_SITES.md).

One native contact can distinguish 64 joint classes of two packet addresses and retain just enough extra information to reverse all 256 inputs under a specified wiring. Yet even with that middle reader, predetermined local address outputs score at most 6 in a two-source functional whose reported values exceed 7. [Detector construction, exact ceiling and experimental scope](scripts/WHEN_TWO_PACKETS_CAN_MAKE_ONE_RECORD.md).

A symmetric binary relation can now *deliver* the four-place operation, not just recognize it: from a clean input, one fixed word of 15 pair offers complements exactly the six half-filled inputs, fixes the other ten, and clears every internal bond. A degree-free alternative also works, but makes changes even on a uniform input. [Exact gates, programming costs and distinguishing tests](scripts/WHEN_A_RELATION_CAN_DELIVER_FOUR_CHANGED_BITS.md).

Three marked edges can move as one pattern; for a different rule two individually immobile patterns form a mobile pair. On a ring, a minus sign for exchanging separated packets is indistinguishable from a spatial twist unless an additional one-packet phase reference is justified. [Raw motion, frozen controls and the exchange/lap comparison](scripts/WHEN_THREE_MARKS_CAN_TRAVEL.md).

For a particle model with no information from the unvisited arm, the four-setting port contrast vanishes when all triggers are counted. The same local-loss table gives 1/2 if clicks are pooled and 13/20 if the four selected fractions are averaged. [Exact loss and memory controls, and what the optical experiment actually measured](scripts/WHAT_A_MISSING_PHOTON_CAN_CHANGE.md).

The pair relation can build its own finite change reader: on an even star, a 51-offer program records whether a source bit changed without undoing the source output or needing an initial copy of the bit. The clean quartet program also transfers to the earlier packet transport and coincidence instruments. [Reader back-action, actual replay coverage and limits](scripts/WHEN_THE_READER_USES_THE_SAME_RELATION.md).

No site marks are necessary for programmed quartet logic: an offered link toggles if either endpoint has an odd number of *other* links. A 30-offer word passes all 16 inputs but two literal glued copies fail; logical bits encoded on disjoint physical edges can instead be composed. [Loopless rule, work-space lower bound, locality controls and composable alternative](scripts/WHEN_LINKS_ALONE_CAN_CHANGE_FOUR_BITS.md).
